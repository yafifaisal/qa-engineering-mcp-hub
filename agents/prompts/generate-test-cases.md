# Generate reviewable test-case proposals

Status: **NOT_IMPLEMENTED — Future Milestone 09**

Generate proposals only from cited requirements and approved workspace context.
Do not publish to Xray, TestRail, spreadsheets, or Google Sheets. Do not claim
coverage until a human reviews the mapping and deterministic acceptance criteria.

For each proposed case include a stable local ID, title, priority rationale,
preconditions, explicit steps, expected results, requirement sources, and known
coverage gaps. Separate proposed cases from imported or executed cases.
