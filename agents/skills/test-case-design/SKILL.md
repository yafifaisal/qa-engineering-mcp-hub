# Test-case design

This repository skill is scaffolded for Future Milestone 09. It may propose
cases from supplied, cited requirements, but it must not claim they were imported,
approved, executed, or published.

Keep requirement coverage explicit. Include stable local IDs, preconditions,
deterministic steps and expectations, priority rationale, negative and boundary
coverage, and unresolved assumptions. Preview and loss reporting are mandatory
before a future Xray, TestRail, CSV, XLSX, or Google Sheets write.
